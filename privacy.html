Privacy Policy — Otter Clicker: Multiverse

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy covers the Otter Clicker: Multiverse app for Android and the web version on CrazyGames, published by Redrik, an independent developer based in Brazil.

What the game stores

The game asks for no sign-up, name, email or password, and sends no data to us. Your progress (coins, buildings, costumes and settings) is saved only on your device. You can delete it with Erase progress in the game's Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The Android version has optional ads: they only play when you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game bonus. There are no banners and no ads between screens. Ads are served by Google AdMob. We always request non-personalized ads, but to show the ad, measure results and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process: IP address (which shows your approximate location), device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), interactions with the ad and diagnostic data. Learn more about how Google uses data from partner apps and in Google's privacy policy.

Consent

Where the law requires it (for example, in the European Union and the United Kingdom), the game shows Google's consent form before any ads. Declining does not stop you from playing. When the form applies to you, you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.

Age

The game is made for ages 13 and up. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens.

Your rights

Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD) and other applicable laws, you can request information, correction or deletion of data. Since we keep no personal data on our own servers, ad data is handled by Google; we can help through the email below. Google may process this data outside Brazil, for the periods set in its own policy.

Web version (CrazyGames)

On CrazyGames, your progress is saved by the CrazyGames save system and, if you sign in, it is tied to your CrazyGames account; the game also tells the platform when play starts and stops. The game does not read your name, email or any other personal data. The web version has no ads. CrazyGames handles site data under the CrazyGames privacy policy.

Google Play

Google Play may process data when you download or update the app, under Google's own privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated and the new version will be on this page.

Contact

Privacy questions: redrikgames@gmail.com
