Privacy Policy — Otter Clicker: Multiverse

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy covers the Otter Clicker: Multiverse app for Android, published by Redrik, an independent developer based in Brazil.

Data we collect

None. The game does not collect, send or share personal data or device data. It has no sign-up or login and does not use the advertising ID.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, buildings, costumes and settings) is saved only on your device. That file never leaves the device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Internet, ads and purchases

The Android version works offline and has no ads, no purchases, no usage analytics and no third-party tools that collect data.

Google Play

Google Play may process data when you download or update the app, under Google's own privacy policy. That processing is Google's, not the game's.

Children

The game is made for ages 13 and up and collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If a future version starts collecting any data (for example, to show ads), this page will be updated first, with the new date at the top.

Contact

Privacy questions: redrikgames@gmail.com
