Otter Clicker: Multiverse

Help the otter build, switch jobs and conquer one world after another.

One otter, one coin and a whole river ahead. Tap, build and help the otter conquer world after world: from River Refuge to Lume Citadel, and from there all the way to The Belt, out in space.

Every building grows in stages right in front of you, gives the otter a new job and a costume to match, and pushes your coins up to giant numbers.

No purchases. Optional ads only when you choose to watch one for a bonus.
