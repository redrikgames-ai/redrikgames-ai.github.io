Otter Clicker: Multiverse

Tap the otter. Build the village. Open a portal.

A relaxed clicker about one very hardworking otter. Every upgrade changes the world you see: a fruit stall, a pottery, a lumber yard and a builder's yard come alive as you invest. Unlock outfits, collect achievements and step through the portal to the Lume Citadel.

Free to play. Ads are optional and only shown if you choose to watch one for a bonus. Coins exist only inside the game.
